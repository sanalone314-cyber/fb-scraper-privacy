Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 18 September 2026

Welcome to FB SCRAPER v1.0. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information our application collects, how it is used, and how it is protected.

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in with Facebook, we may access only the information that you explicitly authorize through Meta Login, including:

  • Facebook User ID
  • Name
  • Public Profile Information
  • Email Address (if permission is granted)
  • Access Token (used only for authorized API requests)

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used only to provide Facebook analytics features inside the application, including:

  • User authentication.
  • Displaying your Facebook profile information.
  • Fetching data that you have authorized via the Meta Graph API.
  • Generating analytics inside your dashboard.

3. Data Storage

FB SCRAPER v1.0 does not sell or share your personal information with third parties. Access tokens are used only for authorized Facebook API requests.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Your information is shared only when required to:

  • Communicate with Meta Graph API.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect authorized user information. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

You may:

  • Revoke Facebook permissions at any time.
  • Request deletion of your stored information.
  • Disconnect your Facebook account from the application.

7. Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your Facebook-related data using our Data Deletion page.

8. Third-Party Services
