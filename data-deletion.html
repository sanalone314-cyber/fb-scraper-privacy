Facebook User Data Deletion Instructions

Data Deletion Request

FB SCRAPER respects your privacy. If you want us to delete the Facebook information associated with your account, you can request deletion at any time.

How to Delete Your Data

  1. Log in to your Facebook account.
  2. Go to Settings → Apps and Websites.
  3. Select FB SCRAPER.
  4. Click Remove.
  5. Facebook will revoke access to our application.

You may also request permanent deletion of your stored data by contacting us.

Email: sanalone314@gmail.com

What Data Will Be Deleted?

  • Facebook User ID
  • Name
  • Profile information accessed through Facebook Login
  • Access tokens associated with your account
  • Any analytics data linked to your Facebook account

Deletion Timeline

We process deletion requests within 7 business days. Once completed, all associated user information is permanently removed from our systems.
